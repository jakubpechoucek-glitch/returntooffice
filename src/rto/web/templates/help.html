Everyone sees their own data. Managers also see everyone below them in the reporting line. The other roles are granted by HR.

Key terms

CommitmentHow many days per week you must be onsite: usually 3 (ExCo-1: 4, ExCo: 5), or less with an approved exemption.
Valid dayA day onsite (door tap in and out), on approved leave, or on approved Official Business. Holidays do not count, and they do not lower the commitment.
Considered weekA Monday–Sunday week with at least 3 working days. The week that spans New Year is never counted.
Week metValid days ≥ commitment. Missed days cannot be made up in another week.
Monthly adherenceWeeks met ÷ considered weeks. The month is met when it reaches your department's threshold (usually 75%).
Annual complianceMonths met ÷ 12. During the year, use the year-to-date figure.
After disputeThe final figure, including approved disputes. Cells outlined in blue were changed by a dispute.

Employee

  1. My dashboard shows your year at a glance. Click a month for the week-by-week and day-by-day view.
  2. Can't come in for specific weeks (medical, family, business)? File an Exemption: choose the weeks and how many days you can do.
  3. Is the published report wrong (for example the turnstile was down, or a leave was not recorded)? Raise a Dispute and enter the day(s).
  4. Has your agreed weekly commitment changed? File a Commitment change from that week onward.
  5. Follow your requests under Requests. They go to your manager first, then to your People Partner. You can withdraw a request while it is pending.

Never write medical details. Select the category “Medical”; that is enough.

Manager

  1. My team lists your direct reports. For managers below you, “Organisation below” shows how their whole team did. Click it to go one level down; the breadcrumb takes you back up.
  2. Requests → Waiting for my decision: approve, or reject with a comment. After your approval the request goes to the People Partner.
  3. You can file requests on behalf of your reports (open the person and use the buttons). Your own manager then approves them.
  4. Reports shows the same data as a table, with Excel/CSV export, limited to your organisation.
  5. Org chart shows your whole reporting line as a tree, with each person's monthly result and, for every manager, how their whole organisation did. Search by name or department.

People Partner

  1. Reports is filtered to your departments. The employee list is grouped by team along the org chart; switch to “Flat list” for one table. Use “Not met only” for follow-ups, and export to Excel for leaders.
  2. Your Requests inbox has requests already approved by the manager. Your approval is final: the figures update immediately.
  3. Use the “By manager” table to see which teams need attention, and click a manager to open their team view.

Executive

Read-only view of your organisation: Reports (department and manager roll-ups, export) and My team for the hierarchy.

HR Analytics: monthly cycle

  1. Admin → Imports: upload the masterlist, then leave/OB, then presence (door, timekeeping, Wi-Fi), or the legacy onsite report. Sample files can be downloaded on that page.
  2. Check the import result: row counts, warnings, and for the legacy report the reconciliation against its own figures.
  3. Review Reports.
  4. Admin → Months → Publish opens the review window (7 days). Managers and employees raise disputes.
  5. When the window closes: Finalize. The month is locked; export the final report for ExCo.
  6. Reporting lines and commitments can be corrected under Admin → People.

HR Admin

  • Thresholds: monthly / annual pass %, minimum working days and default commitment for the company, a department group or one department, from a chosen date. Empty fields inherit from the parent.
  • People: change a person's manager, department, office or commitment with an effective date. History is kept.
  • Organisation units: create, rename or move departments.
  • Roles: People Partner and Executive for specific units; HR Analytics, HR Admin and Auditor company-wide.
  • Calendar: holidays (all offices or one office) and waivers such as typhoons (exclude whole weeks, or treat days like holidays).
  • You can act on any pending approval step (recorded as an override in the audit log).

Auditor

Read-only access to all reports and the audit log: every change, approval, import, export and sign-in, with who and when.

FAQ

Why is my annual compliance so low? It is months met ÷ 12, so it grows during the year. Look at year-to-date.

A holiday was in my week. Do I need fewer days? No. The commitment stays, but a week with fewer than 3 working days is not counted at all.

I worked from another office. Any company office counts.

Who sees my data? You, your managers up the line, your People Partner and HR.
