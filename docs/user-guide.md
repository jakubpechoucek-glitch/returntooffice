# User guide

## Everyone: My dashboard

- **Annual compliance** is months met / 12. During the year this number is naturally low, so the **year to date** tile
  shows how you are tracking.
- **Monthly tiles**: green = month met, red = not met. Click a month for the week-by-week and day-by-day view.
- **Week cells** show `valid days / commitment`. A blue outline means the week was changed by an approved dispute;
  *n/a* means the week is not counted (holidays, year-end week, waiver, before your start date).
- In the **daily strip**, an orange outline marks a day flagged for review. Hover over it to see why, for example
  "INCOMPLETE_TAP" (you tapped in but not out) or "WIFI_ONLY" (no door tap). Wi-Fi-only days do not count; if you
  were in the office, raise a dispute.

## Filing requests

Go to *Requests → New request*, or use the buttons on the dashboard.

| Type | When | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemption** | You cannot meet your commitment in specific weeks (medical, family, business reason) | Start and end date (whole weeks), days per week during the exemption, category |
| **Dispute** | The published report is wrong: you were onsite, on leave or on OB but it is missing, or your commitment was not updated in time | The day(s) to credit, and/or the correct days per week for the period |
| **Commitment change** | Your agreed weekly commitment changes from a certain week onward | The first week and the new days per week |

Requests go to your **direct manager** and then to your **People Partner**. You can follow every step and withdraw a
request while it is pending. Never write medical details; the category "Medical" is enough.

## Managers

- **My team** shows your direct reports. For each manager below you, the *Organisation below* column shows how many
  people in their whole subtree met the month. Click it to go one level down; the breadcrumb takes you back.
- **Org chart** shows the whole reporting line as a collapsible tree: each person's monthly result (hover for
  weeks met and the department threshold) and, for every manager, how everyone below them did. Search by name,
  position or department; HR can jump from any node to *edit* the person's manager or commitment.
- You only see your own reporting line, as of the month you are viewing.
- **Requests → Waiting for my decision**: approve, or reject with a comment. You can also file requests on behalf of
  your reports (the next-level manager then approves).

## People Partners

- *Reports* is filtered to your departments. Use *Not met only* to focus on follow-ups, then *Export Excel*.
- Your *Requests* inbox contains requests already approved by the manager. Your review is the final step: approving
  updates the figures immediately.

## HR Analytics

1. *Admin → Imports*: upload the masterlist, leave/OB and presence files for the period, or the legacy workbook.
   Check the result: row counts, warnings and reconciliation.
2. *Reports*: check the figures.
3. *Admin → Months → Publish* opens the review window (default 7 days); managers and employees can raise disputes.
4. After the window: *Finalize*. The month is locked and the final report is ready for ExCo (*Reports → Export Excel*).

## HR Admin

- **Thresholds**: set monthly / annual pass %, minimum working days and default commitment for the company, a
  department group or a single department, from a given date. Empty fields inherit from the parent.
- **People**: change manager, department or office, or the baseline commitment, with an effective date. History is kept.
- **Organisation units**: create, rename or move departments.
- **Roles**: grant People Partner or Executive roles for specific units; HR roles company-wide.
- **Calendar**: holidays (all offices or one office) and waivers. Waivers either exclude whole weeks or treat
  specific days like holidays (typhoon, office closure).
- **Audit log**: who changed what.
